# Learning evidence and open questions

Research and coverage review, 6 October 2026. The project already covers memory, comprehension, practice, and transfer in depth. Its main practical omission was the conditions under which a self-directed adult starts, sustains, and corrects learning. The additions below address that omission without treating familiar productivity advice as established science.

## The added coverage

| Previously implicit question | Research note | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Why can a different place make it easier to work? | [[Designing the learning environment]] | Situational self-control studies support changing competing opportunities; they do not establish a universal home-versus-library effect. |
| How does an intention become an actual start? | [[Starting and sustaining a learning habit]] | If–then plans have meta-analytic support; effect estimates are sensitive to publication bias. Habit evidence does not supply a universal deadline. |
| What happens when attention is divided or work is interrupted? | [[Attention, interruptions, and breaks]] | Task switching and return planning have experimental evidence; break benefits are clearer for fatigue than performance. |
| What if the task is avoided or feels impossible? | [[Motivation, avoidance, and difficulty]] | Motivation associations, a procrastination account, and guidance experiments are different forms of evidence. |
| How should help and other people fit into learning? | [[Feedback, help, and learning with others]] | Informative feedback and structured peer instruction have support; that does not validate every study group or silent coworking arrangement. |
| How is progress judged and the plan changed? | [[Planning and monitoring learning]] | Monitoring and delayed performance checks have support; a chosen log format is an implementation. |

[[A learning session from start to finish]] makes these components usable together. The combined sequence has not itself been tested.

## Rules for claims in these additions

- State the finding and link the research paper near the claim. A bibliography alone does not establish that a particular sentence follows from it.
- Preserve the population, comparison, duration, and measured outcome when they affect interpretation. A short attention task is not a long-term learning assessment.
- Distinguish randomised interventions, observational associations, reviews, and theoretical explanations. A meta-analysis does not turn correlational inputs into causal evidence.
- Mark practical examples as **applications**. Evidence for an ingredient does not validate every recipe containing it.
- Treat missing evidence as a limit on recommendation, not proof that a practice cannot help anyone.

## Questions that remain open

These are priorities for further investigation, not recommendations being smuggled into the workflow:

1. **Direct comparisons of adult learning settings.** Which aspects of working away from home change initiation, distraction, and delayed learning? The situational-strategy papers do not isolate venue from its accompanying changes.
2. **Durability of changes to study behaviour.** The short field studies in [[Designing the learning environment]] do not show whether gains persist for months or improve independent professional performance.
3. **Silent coworking and accountability.** These require intervention-specific evidence. The peer-discussion findings in [[Feedback, help, and learning with others]] cannot answer them.
4. **Break schedules for demanding adult learning.** The fatigue results in [[Attention, interruptions, and breaks]] do not establish an optimal schedule for retention or complex problem-solving.
5. **The combined system.** Effects from separate studies cannot be added together to predict how much this whole workflow will improve learning.

## Scope of this review

This was a targeted expansion and correction of the learning workflow, not a paper-by-paper revalidation of every older note in the project. The linked additions state their sources and limitations. Existing numerical claims and broad framework verdicts elsewhere should not be assumed newly verified merely because the hub now links to this review.
