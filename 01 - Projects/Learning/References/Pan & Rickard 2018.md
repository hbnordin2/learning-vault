# Pan & Rickard 2018

Pan, S. C., & Rickard, T. C. (2018). Transfer of test-enhanced learning: Meta-analytic review and synthesis. *Psychological Bulletin, 144*(7), 710–756. https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000151

The definitive synthesis of how far the [[The testing effect|testing effect]] transfers (192 transfer effects, 122 experiments). Transfer is real — **d ≈ 0.40** — and robust across changed question *formats* (transfer across formats d ≈ 0.58) and to application/inference items. But the boundary is sharp: transfer to *related-but-untested* material studied alongside the tested content is "especially inconclusive" / near-zero. The corrective to the popular "testing builds broadly flexible knowledge" claim: you get strong benefits for what you actually retrieved and close variants, not for adjacent material you didn't test.
