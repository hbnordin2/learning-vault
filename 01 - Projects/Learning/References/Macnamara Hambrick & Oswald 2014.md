# Macnamara Hambrick & Oswald 2014

Macnamara, B. N., Hambrick, D. Z., & Oswald, F. L. (2014). Deliberate practice and performance in music, games, sports, education, and professions: A meta-analysis. *Psychological Science, 25*(8), 1608–1618. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614535810

Meta-analysis (88 studies) of how much performance variance deliberate practice explains, by domain: **games ~26%, music ~21%, sports ~18%, education ~4%, professions under 1%** — pooled around 12%, dropping to ~5% with high-fidelity prospective practice logs. The decisive counter to the strong "talent is a myth / 10,000 hours" claim: deliberate practice matters but is far from sufficient or dominant, leaving 74–99% of variance to other factors (talent, working memory, starting age). Companion: Macnamara et al. (2016) found that among *elite* athletes deliberate practice explains only ~1%, contradicting "even among elites."
