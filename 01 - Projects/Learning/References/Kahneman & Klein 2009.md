# Kahneman & Klein 2009

Kahneman, D., & Klein, G. (2009). Conditions for intuitive expertise: A failure to disagree. *American Psychologist, 64*(6), 515–526. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016755

A negotiated agreement between a skeptic of expert intuition (Kahneman) and a champion of it (Klein). Their joint conclusion: genuine skilled intuition requires **both** (1) a *high-validity environment* — stable, regular relationships between cues and outcomes — **and** (2) prolonged practice with *rapid, unambiguous feedback*. In low-validity, noisy, delayed-feedback environments (market prediction, long-range strategy, fundraising timing), experience does **not** build expertise; practitioners gain confidence without accuracy. The corollary: subjective confidence and felt experience are not diagnostic of actual skill in such domains.
