# Barnett & Ceci 2002

Barnett, S. M., & Ceci, S. J. (2002). When and where do we apply what we learn? A taxonomy for far transfer. *Psychological Bulletin, 128*(4), 612–637. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.128.4.612

Argues that a century of confused transfer research stalled because researchers compared wildly different "distances" under one label. Provides the standard taxonomy that disciplines any transfer claim: two **content** dimensions (learned skill, performance change, memory demands) and six **context** dimensions — knowledge domain, physical context, temporal context, functional context, social context, and modality. The further a claim moves along these dimensions, the rarer transfer is. Most celebrated "far transfer" successes turn out to be modest hops along a single dimension; transfer that jumps several at once almost never appears.
